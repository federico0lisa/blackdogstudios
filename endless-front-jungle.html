Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 28, 2026

Application: Endless Front: Jungle for Android

This privacy policy describes how Black Dog Studios handles information in Endless Front: Jungle, an offline, single-player game.

1. Information collected by the game

Endless Front: Jungle does not require an account and does not collect or transmit personal information to Black Dog Studios. The game does not use advertising, tracking or analytics services. It does not sell or share personal data collected through gameplay.

2. Local game data

The game stores game preferences, records and earned medals locally on your device to remember your preferences and record. These files are not sent to Black Dog Studios. You can remove local game data by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app. Any backups managed by your device or Google account are subject to the applicable platform settings and policies.

3. Google Play and other services

Google Play may independently process information relating to downloads, purchases, app delivery or platform diagnostics. Black Dog Studios does not receive your payment card information through the game. For information about Google's processing, see Google's Privacy Policy. This policy page is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub may process technical information when you visit it. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

4. Contact and support

If you contact us by email, we use the information you voluntarily provide, including your email address and message, to respond to your request and resolve support issues. We retain correspondence only as long as necessary for these purposes or applicable legal obligations. Where applicable, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or exercise other privacy rights by contacting us. You may also lodge a complaint with your relevant data protection authority.

5. Children's privacy

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other players through gameplay. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to us, please contact us.

6. Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy when the game's functionality or applicable requirements change. The current version will be available on this page.
